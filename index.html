House rules

  1. Each course day: about 1 hour of lessons plus 1–2 hours of practice. Hit your buzzer once it's done.
  2. The team streak only goes up when all three of you buzz in for the day.
  3. Missed a day? Use a streak freeze. If everyone either buzzed in or froze, the streak is held (it doesn't grow, but it doesn't break).
  4. Each player has 2 freezes per week. They come back every Monday.
  5. A day that ends without everyone covered resets the streak to zero. Days off and the holiday break don't count against you.
  6. Forgot to buzz in yesterday? Past days can still be ticked, so own up honestly.
